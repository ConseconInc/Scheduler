⚙️ Shop Scheduler — Quick Setup

This takes 2 minutes. You only do this once. After setup, your whole team just opens the Netlify link — no login, no app, nothing to install.

Step 1 — Go to jsonbin.ioOpen jsonbin.io in a new tab and click Sign Up Free (use Google or email).
Step 2 — Create a binAfter signing in, click + New Bin. In the editor paste exactly this: {"orders":[],"nextId":1} — then click Create Bin.
Step 3 — Copy the Bin IDAfter creating, you'll see a URL like https://api.jsonbin.io/v3/b/abc123xyz. The part after /b/ is your Bin ID. Copy it.
Step 4 — Get your API keyClick your profile icon → API Keys → copy your X-Master-Key.
